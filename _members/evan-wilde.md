Evan Wilde is an MSc student in Biomedical Engineering at the University of Calgary, supervised by Dr. Sarah Manske. He holds a Bachelors of Science in Biology from Niagara University. His research focuses on establishing the link between joint biomechanics and joint structure in trapeziometacarpal (TMC) osteoarthritis, with the goal of enhancing methods to quantify joint loading as it relates to TMC bone microarchitecture using CT. Evan played four years of NCAA baseball during his undergraduate degree, and enjoys being active and playing music in his free time.
